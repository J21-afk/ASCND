OUR STORY

MORE THAN A BIKE SHOP.

Blue Star Bike Shop is part of the ride, the city, and the community.

ABOUT US

Located in the Blue Star Arts Complex, next to the San Antonio River and the San Antonio Mission Trail, you will find the Blue Star Bike Shop. (Less than two miles from both the Alamo and Mission Concepcion). Here, you will find one of San Antonio’s biggest selections of Electra bicycles, BMX, single speeds, fixed gears, Cruisers, Sports Cruisers, Road bikes, city bikes, kids bikes, brooks saddles and bags, along with an extensive showing of accessories to ought your new bike. Whether you’re riding around the neighboring communities (King William, SouthTown, downtown San Antonio or the Missions), or just out for an afternoon ride along the San Antonio River, or even exercise, Blue Star Bike Shop can provide the best in rental gear.

2004

THE BEGINNING.

Owner Joey Villarreal and his wife Magdalena opened the Blue Star Bike Shop within the walls of the brewery. Combining their passion for cycling with their love for the community. The local cycling business becomes part of the Blue Star family.

2009

SERVING THE COMMUNITY.

The bike shop made it's big debut right next door and became a full service bike shop.

TODAY
