Hello and welcome to ASCND's Privacy Policy!

We believe good systems should make business clearer, smarter, and easier to operate. That starts with being transparent about how we handle the information entrusted to us.

This Privacy Policy page explains what information ASCND may collect when you access our website, use our services, or interact with our business assessment tools, and how that information may be used and protected.

1. Overview

ASCND Systems ("ASCND," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website, services, and business assessment tools.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with ASCND, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Business name and business information
  • Information submitted through our business assessment
  • Messages or other information you send to us

We may also collect limited technical information such as browser type, device information, and general website usage data.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and improve ASCND services, evaluate business needs, respond to inquiries, communicate with prospective clients, and improve our website and systems.

Information submitted through the ASCND business assessment may be used to understand your business and identify potential opportunities for digital, operational, or automation improvements.

4. Information Sharing

ASCND does not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers or technology providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, systems, communications, or business services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, security, or users.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your Choises

You may contact ASCND to request information about personal information we may hold about you or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

7. Third Party Services

Our website or services may rely on third-party platforms and services to operate certain functions. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9. Contacts

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or ASCND's handling of information, please contact ASCND through the contact information provided on our website.
